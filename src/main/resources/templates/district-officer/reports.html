District Officer Sanction Authority Scope
Authority & Escalation Boundaries:
  • Regional Authority: District Officers have full sanction, rejection, and escalation authority over all schemes within their designated district.
  • Sanction Forwarding: Approval by District Officer transitions application stage to FINANCE_APPROVAL for Treasury fund disbursement.
  • Reassignment & Anomaly Override: District Officers can assign/reassign cases to Field Officers, resolve anomaly flags, and respond to beneficiary grievances.
