Field Verification Functional Boundaries
Field Officer Operational Scope:
  • Ground Inspection & Document Check: Verify physical existence of beneficiary, Aadhaar, Bank passbook, land/income certificates.
  • Verification Decision: Record approval recommendation or request re-verification.
  • Forwarding: Submitting verification advances case to DISTRICT_REVIEW. Field Officers cannot perform direct financial sanctioning or state treasury dispatch.
