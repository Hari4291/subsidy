Application & Disbursement Process

Transparent 6-Step Direct Benefit Transfer Architecture

Step 1
Beneficiary Enrollment

Register with your full name, Gmail address, 10-digit mobile number, state/district, and Aadhaar/PAN identity card. Form inputs undergo real-time client & server verification.

Step 2
Dynamic Eligibility Scoring

Select your scheme and answer criteria questions. The common rules engine calculates your eligibility score out of 100 points without hardcoded code constraints.

Step 3
Online Application

If eligible (score ≥ 60/100), click "Apply Now" to submit your grant request. An official tracking ID e.g. APP-2026-XXXXX is instantly generated.

Step 4
Field Officer Inspection

Authorized Field Verification Officers conduct ground inspections and document scrutiny. They can approve, request re-verification, or reject with reason.

Step 5
District Collectorate Review

District Officers review application details, field officer remarks, and regional budget allocations before sanctioning the grant request.

Step 6
Staged Milestone Release

Finance Approvers authorize fund release. Grants are disbursed in scheduled milestone installments (e.g. 30% - 40% - 30%) with compliance tracking.
