Citizen Helpdesk & Frequently Asked Questions

To ensure communication security and account verification, the portal accepts valid @gmail.com email addresses exclusively. Other domain addresses will be rejected during registration validation.

The portal displays the national code prefix +91. You must enter exactly 10 digits starting with 6, 7, 8, or 9. The database stores the clean 10-digit number without country prefix.

Addresses must be 10 to 200 characters in length. Only alphanumeric characters (A-Z, a-z, 0-9), spaces, forward slash (/), and hyphen (-) are accepted. Special characters such as @, $, %, &, *, _, +, !, commas, and periods are stripped immediately on typing.

Once your application is approved by the Finance Approver, grants are split into staged milestones (e.g. 30% Milestone 1, 40% Milestone 2, 30% Milestone 3). Milestone funds are released upon verified completion of compliance benchmarks.
